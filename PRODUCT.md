# BuildGraph
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->
## Platform
web
## Stack
User-specified React/TypeScript, Three.js, Python/FastAPI, Pydantic, NumPy/SciPy, Shapely, OR-Tools, server-side Gemini, local persistence and Google Cloud adapters.
## Users
Small Philippine architectural and engineering teams exploring one-floor building concepts and iterating a modest single-storey coordinated building.
## Product Purpose
Let an architectural design agent develop programme, site strategy and detailed spatial layouts, with explicit user acceptance before engineering inputs, deterministic screens, specialist review and traceable review outputs. Supported coordinated-building edits also remain available in the template workspace.
## Operating Context
Desktop engineering workspace; review at intermediate stages; synthetic projects available without credentials.
## Constraints
Staged polygon planning supports one floor and footprints up to 500 m across. Detailed objects, measured routes, sourced inputs and bounded component/arithmetic screens follow explicit design acceptance; whole-building engineering and statutory determinations remain unsupported. The coordinated engine retains its regular orthogonal family up to 30 × 30 m. Large or alternative concepts are never silently converted to engineering models. Professional review required for construction. No fabricated compliance, rates, solver results or live AI calls.
## Principles
Candidates never overwrite the accepted revision. Missing inputs cannot pass. Sources and assumptions travel with results. Users can compare, undo, export and resume. Specialist agents share the same accepted design and input hashes; their advice cannot change state or replace deterministic findings.
## Assumptions
Use the task's dedicated directory. First example is a 12 × 9 m building, a central accessible corridor, six rooms and a regular steel roof grid. All dimensions, loads and rates are synthetic. Local single-user prototype; production authentication is an integration requirement.
