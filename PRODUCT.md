# BuildGraph
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->
## Platform
web
## Stack
User-specified React/TypeScript, Three.js, Python/FastAPI, Pydantic, NumPy/SciPy, Shapely, OR-Tools, server-side Gemini, local persistence and Google Cloud adapters.
## Users
Small Philippine architectural and engineering teams iterating a modest single-storey building.
## Product Purpose
Make a real user edit propagate through geometry, deterministic calculations, service routes, checks and traceable quantities before acceptance.
## Operating Context
Desktop engineering workspace; review at intermediate stages; synthetic projects available without credentials.
## Constraints
One regular orthogonal building family; explicit partial analytical scope. Professional review required for construction. No fabricated compliance, rates, solver results or live AI calls.
## Principles
Candidates never overwrite the accepted revision. Missing inputs cannot pass. Sources and assumptions travel with results. Users can compare, undo, export and resume.
## Assumptions
Use the task's dedicated directory. First example is a 12 × 9 m building, a central accessible corridor, six rooms and a regular steel roof grid. All dimensions, loads and rates are synthetic. Local single-user prototype; production authentication is an integration requirement.
